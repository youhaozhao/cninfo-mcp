Guides ​

Each guide covers one research task and includes prompts you can paste into your assistant. If you have not installed the server yet, start with Install and configure.

  • Read Chinese annual reports with AI: how the assistant reads a 143-page report through its table of contents, keyword search and page ranges, and answers in English with page references.
  • Download annual reports of Chinese listed companies: save every annual report a company has filed with one request, for research or text analysis.
  • Is there a CNINFO API?: the official data service, the website's own endpoints, scrapers and MCP, and when to use which.

More guides are available in Chinese: searching announcements and investor-relations records, financial and shareholder data, IPO prospectuses and choosing an A-share MCP server.

For every tool and parameter, see the tools reference.
